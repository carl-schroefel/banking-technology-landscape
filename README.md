# 🏦 Banking Technology Landscape

*A practical map of the technologies, architectures and regulatory drivers shaping modern banking IT.*

This repository reflects how I structure and understand the technology landscape I encounter when working with IT organizations in banking.

I am not an engineer or architect. My role is to understand the challenges behind technology projects well enough to have meaningful conversations with the people responsible for them.

---

## 🗺️ The Landscape

### 🏦 Core Banking & Digital Platforms
`Core Banking` `Digital Banking` `API Banking` `Legacy Modernization` `Microservices`

How traditional banking platforms connect with modern digital services and architectures.

### 💳 Payments
`SEPA` `Instant Payments` `SWIFT` `ISO 20022` `APIs` `Fraud Detection`

From payment infrastructure and messaging standards to modern real-time payment architectures.

### ☁️ [Cloud & Infrastructure](./cloud/README.md)
`Microsoft Azure` `AWS` `Kubernetes` `Containers` `Platform Engineering` `Hybrid Cloud`

Cloud adoption in banking is not only a technology question. Resilience, security, governance and regulatory requirements shape architecture decisions.

### 📊 Data & AI
`Data Platforms` `Data Governance` `Data Lineage` `Databricks` `Snowflake` `Kafka` `GenAI`

Data architecture connects technology with risk, reporting, analytics and increasingly AI.

### 🔐 Cyber Security
`IAM` `PAM` `Zero Trust` `SOC` `SIEM` `DevSecOps`

Identity, access, monitoring and resilience are fundamental parts of modern banking technology.

### 🏗️ Architecture
`Enterprise Architecture` `Solution Architecture` `Integration` `Event-Driven Architecture` `APIs`

The layer connecting business capabilities, applications, data and infrastructure.

### ⚙️ Software Engineering
`Java` `.NET` `Python` `CI/CD` `DevOps` `Microservices` `Cloud Native`

The engineering capabilities behind modern banking applications and platforms.

### ⚖️ Regulation & Technology
`DORA` `MaRisk` `BCBS 239` `ICT Risk` `Third-Party Risk` `Operational Resilience`

Banking technology does not exist separately from regulation. Architecture, cloud, data and outsourcing decisions increasingly have to be understood together with regulatory requirements.
